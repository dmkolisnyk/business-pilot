# Business Pilot Product Skill

Use this skill when shaping product scope, MVP decisions, or feature behavior for Business Pilot.

## Workflow

1. Start from the product goal: help WooCommerce store owners increase revenue, recover lost revenue, and choose concrete next actions.
2. Confirm the feature fits the MVP: WooCommerce connection, sync, analytics, AI report, recommendations, customer segments, email drafts, or competitor tracking.
3. Reject generic dashboard work unless it answers a specific business question.
4. Define the store-owner outcome, required data, expected UI/API surface, and any follow-up documentation.

## Rules

- Treat Business Pilot as a B2B SaaS with multi-tenant data isolation.
- Keep WooCommerce first; future integrations must not shape MVP complexity.
- Do not add business logic before architecture, data model, and security rules are clear.
- Prefer practical, explainable features over broad automation.

## Output

Return concise product requirements, acceptance criteria, risks, and recommended next steps.
